## Rebuild Thesis

The presentation should argue that the titular memorial is not a sequence of CV items. It is the consolidation of one academic method:

> experimental magnetism + thermodynamic modeling + formation of people + institutional construction + international leadership.

Every major section should answer one question:

- Formacao: where did this method come from?
- UFF: how did it become institutional work?
- Resultados: what physics did it produce?
- Impacto: how did the work circulate and gain recognition?
- Internacionalizacao: how did the networks become structural rather than episodic?
- Futuro: what changes with the responsibility of a professor titular?

#### 4.1 Tsallis and magnetism

Card units:

- Problem: CMR manganites and magnetic non-homogeneity; traditional mean-field descriptions fail to fit systematic deviations.
- Formalism: Tsallis entropy, q-exponential, generalized Brillouin/Langevin functions.
- Observable: `q` as a physically interpretable measure of magnetic inhomogeneity.
- Result: M(H) curves in `La0.60Y0.07Ca0.33MnO3`; 175-290 K above `Tc = 150 K`; generalized Langevin fit.
- Contribution: evidence of non-extensivity in CMR manganites; direct experimental determination of `q`; non-extensive magnetic phase diagram.

Equation candidates:

- `S_q = k_B(1 - sum_i p_i^q)/(q - 1)`.
- `e_q(x) = [1 + (1 - q)x]^{1/(1-q)}`.
- `M_q = N g mu_B <S_z>_q`.

Visual candidates:

- Figure 4.1 M(H) in manganite.

Footer refs:

- MEM 4.1; refs. [2-8], especially [3-7].

#### 4.2 Magnetocaloric effect and magnetic materials

Card units:

- Problem: magnetic field changes entropy or adiabatic temperature; basis for solid-state magnetic refrigeration.
- Thermodynamics: isothemal `Delta S_mag`; adiabatic `Delta T_ad`; Maxwell relation; total heat capacity split into magnetic/lattice/electronic terms.
- Materials: manganites, intermetallics, anisotropic systems, molecular complexes, multiferroic composites, graphene.
- Mechanisms: chemical substitution, phase separation, anisotropy, spin reorientation, inverse MCE, magnetostructural transitions.
- Contribution: combined experimental/theoretical mapping of mechanisms and review on metal-complex magnetocaloric/barocaloric effects.

Equation candidates:

- `Delta S_mag(T, Delta B) = int_{B_i}^{B_f} (partial M / partial T)_B dB`.
- Maxwell relation `(partial M / partial T)_B = (partial S_mag / partial B)_T`.
- `Delta T_ad(T, Delta B) = - int_{B_i}^{B_f} [T / c_tot(T,B)] (partial M / partial T)_B dB`.
- `c_tot(T,B) = c_mag(T,B) + c_lat(T)`.

Visual candidates:

- If no single MCE figure is in chapter 4, use a mechanism card/schematic in C9/C10 and cite MEM-p025 to MEM-p027.

Footer refs:

- MEM 4.2; refs. [9-23].

#### 4.3 Metal complexes, magnetism and thermodynamic modeling

Card units:

- Problem: macroscopic magnetism emerges from spin centers, ligands, exchange, anisotropy and crystal symmetry.
- Formalism: Heisenberg exchange, zero-field splitting, Zeeman term, optional Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya.
- Method: diagonalize Hamiltonian, get energy spectrum, partition function, Helmholtz free energy, magnetization and susceptibility.
- CARDAMOMO: automated fitting for zero- and one-dimensional molecular magnets; clusters/chains; unimetallic/bimetallic models; Ising options.
- Representative result: `Na2Cu5Si4O14` zig-zag dimer-trimer chain; Cu(II) s=1/2; exchange constants `J1 = -224.9 K`, `J3 = 40.22 K`, `J2 = -8.01 K`, `g = 2.30`; homometallic ferrimagnetism.
- Contribution: reproducible bridge between structure, microscopic Hamiltonian and fitted thermodynamic observables.

Equation candidates:

- `H_ex = - sum_<i,j> J_ij S_i . S_j`.
- `H_ani = D sum_i S_{i,z}^2`.
- `H_Z = - mu_B sum_i g_i S_i . B`.
- `H = H_ex + H_ani + H_Z`.
- `Z = sum_n exp(-E_n/k_B T)` and derived `F`, `M`, `chi`.
- Cluster Hamiltonian Eq. 4.13 for `Na2Cu5Si4O14`.

Visual candidates:

- Figure 4.2 chain structure.

Footer refs:

- MEM 4.3; refs. [24-36], especially [26,27,35].

#### 4.4 Quantum information in magnetic materials

Card units:

- Problem: investigate entanglement and quantum correlations in real magnetic systems, not only idealized qubits.
- Method: use magnetic susceptibility as an experimental entanglement witness.
- Results:

  - thermal entanglement hundreds of Kelvin above room temperature in iron dimers;
  - spin-orbit thermal entanglement in rare-earth ions;
  - carboxylate-based molecular magnets as room-temperature quantum-correlation platforms.
- Contribution: bridge between condensed matter measurements and quantum-information concepts.

#### 4.5 Relativistic materials and caloric effects

Card units:

- Problem: graphene has relativistic electronic spectrum; Landau quantization changes thermodynamic response.
- Result: oscillating magnetocaloric effect in graphene.
- Mechanism: entropy oscillations vs field, analogous to de Haas-van Alphen; characteristic temperature around 100 K, much higher than conventional 3D diamagnets around 1 K.
- Extension: electrocaloric effect, electric-field influence on oscillating MCE, nanoribbons, multilayer graphene, specific heat, electric/thermal conductivity, barocaloric effect.
- Contribution: connect Dirac fermions in 2D systems to macroscopic caloric effects.

Equation candidates:

Visual candidates:

#### 4.6 Quantum thermodynamics applied to new materials
