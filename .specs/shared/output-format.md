# Output Format

## Primary Deliverable

A polished memorial presentation for the professor titular examination at Instituto de Fisica da UFF.

## Preferred MVP Outputs

- Static HTML presentation for review and live presenting.
- PDF export for stable sharing and archiving.

## Optional Output

- PPTX export if the user needs editable PowerPoint.

## Suggested Narrative Sections

The final structure should be confirmed after source extraction, but the expected memorial arc is:

1. Opening: identity, purpose, and memorial thesis.
2. Formation and academic trajectory.
3. Research agenda and scientific contributions.
4. Teaching, mentoring, and human formation.
5. Institutional service, leadership, and collective contribution.
6. Integration: what the trajectory says about titular readiness.
7. Future vision for IF-UFF, research, teaching, and service.
8. Closing statement.

## Slide Anatomy

Each slide should have:

- One main message.
- Minimal supporting text.
- Source linkage in notes or visible footer.
- Visual support when it clarifies the argument.
- Speaker note when context is too dense for the slide.

## Design Direction

- Academic, mature, and clear.
- High contrast and projector-safe.
- Avoid decorative excess.
- Prefer diagrams, timelines, maps, and evidence-backed visuals.
- Keep each slide readable from a distance.

## Delivery Quality Bar

The final deck should feel like a titular memorial presentation, not a converted CV. It should present a coherent argument about trajectory, contribution, maturity, and future institutional value.
