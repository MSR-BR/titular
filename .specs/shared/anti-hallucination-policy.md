# Anti-Hallucination Policy

## Core Rule

No unsourced factual content may enter the final deck. A strong memorial presentation should be persuasive because the evidence is organized well, not because unsupported claims are added.

## Required Workflow

1. Extract from sources before drafting factual slides.
2. Register evidence before using it in the deck.
3. Draft slide claims from registered evidence.
4. Mark uncertain items as `needs_review`.
5. Remove or revise any claim that cannot be verified.

## Assumption Handling

Assumptions must be written explicitly in the relevant spec, evidence registry, or slide note. Assumptions are allowed for workflow decisions, such as file format or slide count, but not for factual claims about the memorial.

## High-Risk Content

The following require special checking:

- Publication counts and citation metrics.
- Dates of degrees, positions, promotions, and awards.
- Names of collaborators, students, programs, committees, and institutions.
- Research impact claims.
- Funding, grants, patents, prizes, and public policy influence.
- Comparisons against peers or national benchmarks.

## AI Use Rules

- AI may help organize, rewrite, condense, and design.
- AI may not invent missing facts.
- AI may not fabricate references.
- AI may not infer prestige, impact, or intent unless the source supports it.
- AI-generated summaries must be checked against extracted text.

## Validation Gates

No final export should be accepted unless:

- All factual slides have source links.
- All unresolved assumptions are listed.
- The deck has no placeholder claims.
- The output was reviewed against the memorial objective.
- The user has a clear list of any remaining uncertainties.
